Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

The short version

Slot Beasts collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics that leave your device. Your game progress is saved only on your device, and we never receive it.

What is saved on your device

The game keeps a small amount of data in your device's local storage so you can pick up where you left off:

  • Progress: stars, runs played, titles won, best fighter total, date last played, title streak, and which one-time tips you have already seen.
  • Tutorial progress and the timer for your daily free respin.
  • Settings: sound, vibration and language.
  • A random player code the game may create for online play. Online play is not available in this version, so the code is never sent anywhere.

None of this includes your name, email, contacts, location, photos or device identifiers. It stays on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Offline play

To work without a connection, the game stores copies of its own files (code, art and these pages) in your device's app cache. The cache holds game files only, never information about you.

What we do not do

  • No sign-in and no account of any kind.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or crash reporting sent off your device.
  • No cookies, and no third-party tracking or software development kits.
  • No game server: this version plays entirely on your device.

Sharing a result

When you tap Share, the game draws an image of your result on your device and hands it to your device's share menu, or saves it as a file. Nothing is uploaded by the game. Whatever you share is then handled by the app you choose and its own privacy policy.

Where you got the game

The game's files are downloaded from the app store or website you installed it from. Those services (for example Apple, Google or the website host) may record standard technical details such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. We do not receive that information from them.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its data in your device or browser settings. This removes your progress permanently; we hold no copy to restore it from.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that sends data off your device, such as online play, ads or analytics, we will update this policy first and ask for your permission wherever the law requires it. The date at the top shows the latest version.
